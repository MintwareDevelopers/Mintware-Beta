# Reviewing Transactions

Mintware is designed to explain important on-chain actions before your wallet opens.

---

## What You May See Before Confirming

Before certain actions, Mintware may show:
- the asset you are sending
- the asset you expect to receive
- the network the action will happen on
- the estimated network fee
- route or execution context
- warnings for unusual price impact or missing gas balance

---

## Approval vs Final Action

Some actions involve two separate steps:

1. Giving a contract permission to use a token
2. Completing the final action, such as funding, depositing, or swapping

Mintware aims to explain those steps separately so token permissions are not confused with actual transfers.

---

## Safer Multi-Step Actions

For higher-trust actions like campaign funding, claims, and vault deposits, Mintware is moving toward:
- clearer step-by-step progress
- better recovery guidance
- fewer unnecessary approval prompts
- stronger checks before submission

---

## Why This Matters

Wallet prompts are important, but they are often not enough on their own. Mintware adds product-level context before the wallet popup so users can make faster and more confident decisions.
