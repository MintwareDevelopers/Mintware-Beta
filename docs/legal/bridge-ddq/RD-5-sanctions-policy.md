## 1. Reliance on Bridge/Stripe sanctions-screening infrastructure

Per Bridge/Stripe's confirmation (2026-09), sanctions screening for this program is performed using
Bridge/Stripe's own infrastructure, including screening against the standard sanctions lists applicable
to the program (US OFAC SDN list, UN Security Council sanctions lists, UK HM Treasury sanctions list, and
relevant local lists), at onboarding and on an ongoing basis as those lists are updated. Mintware relies
on this screening rather than operating an independent, parallel sanctions-list-matching system.

Mintware does not support or conduct transactions involving sanctioned countries or persons (Iran, Syria,
Cuba, North Korea, or the Crimea, Donetsk, or Luhansk regions, or any other jurisdiction subject to
comprehensive US sanctions).

## 2. Risk tolerance for sanctions-tainted digital assets

Mintware's program operates exclusively in USDC — a centrally-issued, well-established dollar
stablecoin, not a privacy-focused or anonymity-enhanced asset. **Mintware's risk tolerance for
sanctions-tainted coins or tokens is zero**: any wallet, transaction, or counterparty identified as
sanctions-tainted (whether via Bridge/Stripe's screening or Mintware's own review) is not permitted to
transact within the program, and any such instance is escalated immediately per the process below.

## 3. Escalation and internal point of contact

[MLRO NAME] serves as Mintware's internal point of contact for sanctions-compliance matters relating to
this program, responsible for coordinating with Bridge/Stripe/Lead Bank on any flagged wallet, customer,
or transaction, and for maintaining this policy document.

**Annual training.** Mintware commits to annual sanctions-compliance training for all personnel with
access to customer onboarding, card issuance, or compliance escalation functions.
