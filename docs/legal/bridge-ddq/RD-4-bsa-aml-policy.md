## 1. Reliance on Bridge/Stripe compliance infrastructure

Mintware's card-issuing program operates on a bank-sponsored, program-manager model via Bridge/Stripe
Issuing, sponsored by Lead Bank. Per Bridge/Stripe's confirmation (2026-09), the following AML/BSA
functions are performed using Bridge/Stripe's own compliance infrastructure, not a Mintware-built system:

- Know Your Customer (KYC) identity verification on all customers
- Verification of the accuracy of KYC information collected
- Risk-rating of customers (geographic risk, PEP status, transactional/behavioral red flags, product
  usage)
- Ongoing transaction monitoring for suspicious activity
- Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) and related regulatory filing obligations, which sit with Bridge/
  Stripe/Lead Bank as the regulated party in this program structure

Mintware relies on this infrastructure as its issuing partner's compliance program and does not operate
a parallel, independent KYC/AML detection system.

## 2. Mintware's own internal accountability layer

Independent of the underlying screening infrastructure, Mintware maintains its own internal policy and
accountability structure, described below.

**Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO).** [MLRO NAME] is designated as Mintware's Money Laundering
Reporting Officer, responsible for: serving as Mintware's internal point of contact for AML/BSA matters
relating to this program; escalating and coordinating with Bridge/Stripe/Lead Bank on any compliance
inquiry or flagged activity; and maintaining this policy document.

**Escalation process.** Where Bridge/Stripe's monitoring flags a customer or transaction for review,
Mintware's MLRO is notified and coordinates Mintware's response (e.g. providing additional business
context about the customer relationship) within the timeframe Bridge/Stripe requires.

**Record-keeping.** Mintware retains records of customer organizations, their members, and card issuance
decisions within its own systems, available to support Bridge/Stripe/Lead Bank's compliance reviews on
request.

**Annual training.** Mintware commits to annual AML/CFT training for all personnel with access to
customer onboarding, card issuance, or compliance escalation functions, approved by Mintware's governing
body per the sign-off below.
