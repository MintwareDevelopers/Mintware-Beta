# Leaderboard

The Mintware leaderboard ranks participants within each campaign by points earned. It updates in real time as swaps and actions are credited.

---

## How Rankings Work

Each campaign has its own leaderboard. Your rank is determined by your total points accumulated within that campaign — not your Attribution score.

Points are earned through campaign actions:
- **Trade** — points credited per qualifying swap, once per calendar day
- **Bridge** — one-time points for bridging into the campaign chain
- **Referral activity** — points credited when wallets you referred complete actions

In Points Campaigns, your raw points are weighted by your **score multipliers** (Attribution + Sharing) at credit time. Higher multipliers mean more points per action — and a higher leaderboard rank.

---

## Switching Campaigns

Use the campaign selector at the top of the leaderboard to switch between active campaigns. Each campaign maintains a fully independent ranking.

---

## Your Rank

When your wallet is connected, your rank and points total appear at the top of the page. If you haven't joined the selected campaign yet, your rank won't appear — join from the campaign detail page first.

---

## Pool Remaining

The leaderboard also shows the current pool remaining and days left in the campaign. This helps you gauge reward density as the campaign progresses — earlier participation means more pool to compete for.

---

## Epoch Settlement

At the end of each epoch, the leaderboard snapshot is used to compute the Merkle distribution. Your rank at settlement time determines your payout — not your peak rank during the epoch. Consistent daily activity is more valuable than a single large trade.
