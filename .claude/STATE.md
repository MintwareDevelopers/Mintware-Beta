## Public site map

The public marketing site uses one light design system and the owner-approved vision copy. Primary routes are
`/`, `/markets`, `/rwas`, `/issuers`, `/cards`, and `/agents`; the header carries the Testnet chip and
links to the app. **One app (2026-10-31):** every "Launch app" goes to `/app/markets`; the old `/v1` platform
and `/markets/[pool]` redirect into `/app`. The app has two workspaces in one sidebar shell (`components/web2/AppShell.tsx`):
**Personal** (open to everyone) — Markets (`/app/markets`, pool detail `/app/markets/[pool]`), Portfolio
(`/app/portfolio`), Swap (`/app/swap`, LI.FI), the RWA property demo (`/app/rwas`), and Previews: Card, Agents and
Issuers (`/app/previews/*`, Simulated, tagged on screen). **Team** (`/app/team`, `/app/org/*`) stays behind the V2
access code. Earn (the stablecoin vault) is presented as the card's
earning balance, not a standalone page. Legacy `/about`, `/earn`, `/earn/[pool]`, `/vaults`, `/defi`, `/teams`,
`/solutions/:path*`, `/yield-payment-network`, and `/explorer` redirect to their current destinations. Legal and
risk content remain on their existing routes. Canonical route inventory is generated in the AUTO block below.

## Current products and status

- **V1 liquidity: MarketEngine** — one engine per curated Uniswap v4 pool, with one quote-valued share token,
  per-token ERC-4626 adapters, guarded lifecycle operations, and in-kind withdrawals. Robinhood Chain testnet is
  live with a fixed test mark and idle-custody adapters (held ready, no yield); Base Sepolia is also deployed.
  Testnet-only, unaudited; external audit gates real value. See [`rules/lp-gateway.md`](rules/lp-gateway.md).
- **Earn** — `MintwareYieldVault` (`mwEARN`) is a single-source ERC-4626 vault. Pausing blocks deposits only;
  illiquid exits pay what is obtainable, leaving unpaid shares with the holder. See [`rules/payments-ypn.md`](rules/payments-ypn.md).
- **Card refill** — `MintwarePaymentGateway` is refill-only: an authorized relayer redeems a member's Earn shares
  to that member's registered buffer. It does not settle purchases or spend from vault NAV. The current card
  integration is Bridge + Stripe in pre-enable state; see [`rules/payments-ypn.md`](rules/payments-ypn.md).
- **Discover and curation** — GeckoTerminal data feeds candidate discovery; risk scores rank candidates and never
  certify them. Curated MarketEngine instances are verified against on-chain configuration.
- **RWA demo** — the property demo (`/app/rwas`) and `MintwareRwaAppraisalHook` remain a testnet demo. See
  [`rules/rwa-liquidity.md`](rules/rwa-liquidity.md).
- **Attribution** — the AIAttribution v3 contract is deployed on Base mainnet; this is a fundless attestation
  ledger. User-facing agent reputation surfaces remain documented in [`rules/agents.md`](rules/agents.md).

## Retired or parked

The former LP Gateway mechanics (PositionManager/Staging, stage/deploy/harvest/compound/restake automation,
fee ledger, cost-basis RPCs, portfolio and leaderboard surfaces) are retired. Legacy `gateway_*` route and table
names remain for MarketEngine registry, curation, discovery, and receipt-verified transaction events. The old
Gateway rule was replaced with the current MarketEngine reference.

Arc/CCTP settlement, Lithic, spend-in-place YPN, campaigns, Treasury senior/junior new issuance, matched launch,
lock tiers, and ULV/JIT/MEV as a live product are retired or parked. MarketEngine V3/JIT is parked. Do not present
these as current functionality. Historic reports remain historical records.

**Status everywhere:** testnet, unaudited; external audit gates real value. Do not use deposit/savings/guaranteed/
fixed-APY framing.
